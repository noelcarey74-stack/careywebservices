How it works

A simple website process from start to finish.

The aim is to make the whole thing straightforward. You send what you have, I help turn it into a clean website.

Quick chat

We talk about what you do, who your customers are and what the website needs to do.

Gather content

You send your business name, photos, services, contact details and any prices or reviews.

Build and polish

I build the pages, tidy the wording and make sure the site is clear on phone and desktop.

Go live
