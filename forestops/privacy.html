Effective date: 2 November 2026. ForestOps Pro is a forestry operator reporting application developed by Carey Web Services in Cork, Ireland.

1. What remains on your phone

Job sites, property and reference numbers, operator and contractor contacts, timber quantities, machine hours, supplies, hydraulic hose details, notes and optional selected photographs are saved on your device. You can record work offline. We do not collect your GPS location or microphone recordings through the reporting features.

2. Optional cloud backup and account

If you choose to register or sign in for backup, we process your email address and authentication credentials through Supabase Authentication. If you turn on backup or request an upload, copies of your saved profile, job sites, reports and attached photos are sent over HTTPS to a private cloud snapshot associated with your account. Backups are access-controlled by user identity. If you do not use cloud backup, your reports remain local.

3. Purchases and subscriptions

On iPhone, Apple handles StoreKit subscriptions. On Android, Google Play handles subscription purchases, billing, payment confirmation and renewals. ForestOps Pro receives and checks the platform's purchase status to provide Premium features. We do not receive or store complete payment-card details.

4. Sharing and photographs

You choose when to share a PDF using your phone's sharing interface. The receiving app or service you select—such as Mail, Messages or WhatsApp—then processes the file under its own terms. Photos are selected using the device's system picker and included only with the corresponding report/PDF or optional cloud backup. ForestOps Pro does not upload your entire Photos library.

5. Purpose, retention and security

We use report data to provide local reporting, customer-directed PDF sharing and optional backup/restore; account data is used for authentication; purchase status is used to verify access. We do not sell your personal data or use it for behavioural advertising. Active cloud snapshots are retained while associated with your account and may be deleted through the in-app account deletion process, subject to platform-level backup retention and legal requirements. Reports kept locally remain on the device until you remove them or delete the app and its local data. Network transfer uses HTTPS.

6. Your choices and rights

You can edit or delete local reports within the app, opt out of optional automatic backups, sign out of cloud backup, or delete your cloud account. To delete your cloud identity and stored snapshots, open Settings → Delete Account, read the warning and type DELETE to confirm. This does not cancel Apple or Google Play subscriptions and does not erase reports already stored on your phone. See full deletion instructions. If you are in the EU, you may also have rights to access, correct, export, erase or restrict personal data. Contact us to exercise these rights.

7. Service providers

Supabase is used for optional account authentication and cloud backups. Apple handles iOS subscriptions; Google Play handles Android subscriptions. The device operating system provides photo selection and sharing. Optional third-party sharing apps process files only when you choose to send them.

8. Contact and changes

For a privacy request, email careywebservicesireland@gmail.com. We may update this page when the app or service changes; the date above shows the current version.
