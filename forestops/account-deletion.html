ForestOps Pro lets you delete your account within the app without emailing support.

Delete your ForestOps Pro cloud account

  1. Open ForestOps Pro on your iPhone.
  2. Go to Settings and sign in if required.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Read the account-deletion and Apple billing warnings.
  5. Type DELETE and tap Delete Account and Cloud Backups.

The account deletion process removes your Supabase account and associated cloud backup snapshots. It does not erase local job sites or PDFs already saved on this phone or copies shared with others.

Cancel an Apple subscription separately

Deleting your account does not cancel billing. Open Apple Subscriptions to manage or cancel your subscription.

Erase local data

Delete individual local reports in the app, or delete ForestOps Pro and its data from your iPhone. Deleting the app does not itself delete cloud backups: use the in-app Delete Account option first.

Need assistance?

If you cannot sign in or access the app, email careywebservicesireland@gmail.com with the account email address. Do not send passwords or sign-in codes.
