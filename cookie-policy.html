Cookie Policy

This page explains how cookies and similar technologies may be used on careywebservices.ie.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that may be placed on your device when you visit a website. They can help a website work properly, remember preferences, or understand how visitors use a website.

2. Current cookie use on this website

Carey Web Services is currently a simple information website. It is designed to show website packages, sample websites, business extras, contact details and service information.

At this stage, this website does not need cookies for online shopping, customer accounts, stock systems or payment processing because Carey Web Services does not run an online shop on this site.

3. Essential cookies

Some websites may use essential cookies or technical storage to make the site work correctly. These are normally used for basic functions such as security, page loading or remembering basic settings.

4. Analytics cookies

Carey Web Services may add analytics in the future to understand how visitors find and use the website. Analytics can help show which pages are useful, such as packages, sample websites, business extras or contact pages.

If analytics cookies or tracking tools are added, this Cookie Policy may be updated and a suitable cookie notice may be added where required.

5. Advertising and tracking cookies

Carey Web Services does not currently use advertising pixels or advanced tracking cookies on this website. If advertising tools are added in the future, this policy will be updated.

6. Third-party services

Some links on this website may take you to third-party services, such as:

  • Email
  • WhatsApp
  • Phone links
  • Facebook, Instagram or X links, where added
  • Google Business Profile, Google Reviews or Google Maps, if added
  • Domain or hosting services, where relevant
  • Design or PDF tools used for QR code cards, review cards or flyers, where relevant

These third-party services may use their own cookies or tracking technologies. Carey Web Services does not control cookies used by external websites.

7. Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block cookies, delete cookies or receive alerts before cookies are placed on your device.

8. Updates to this Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy may be updated if Carey Web Services adds analytics, advertising tools, booking tools, forms or other services that use cookies.
