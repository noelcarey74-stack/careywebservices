How It Helps

Why QR codes and review cards are useful

A customer might not remember a web address, but they can scan a QR code. That makes it easier for them to find your website, save your details or leave a review.

Easy for customers

Customers can scan a card and open your website, Google review page or contact details without typing anything.

Looks professional

A clean card, flyer or review card makes a small service business look sharper and more prepared.

Helps build trust

Google reviews, contact buttons and a proper website all help new customers feel more confident.

Simple Process

What the customer sends

To create QR code cards, review cards, flyers or sponsor cards, the customer only needs to send the basic details.

1

Business details

Business name, phone number, email, website address, services and area covered.

2

Links

Website link, Facebook link, Instagram link, X link or Google review link if available.

3

Style

Logo, preferred colours, photos or a simple clean design if the business does not have branding yet.

4

PDF file

The final design can be supplied as a PDF file for printing or sharing with customers.
