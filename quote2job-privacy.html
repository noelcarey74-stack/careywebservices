Privacy Policy

This policy explains the information Quote2Job processes, why it is used and the choices available to you.

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Information Quote2Job processes

Quote2Job may process account information used for sign-in, your business profile, customer contact details, job-site information, quotes, invoices, notes, payment records that you enter, and content you choose to type, dictate, photograph or scan. Business branding images may also be stored when you use branding features.

How information is used

Your information is used to provide your account, organise customers and jobs, create quotes and invoices, generate PDFs, process Quote2Job Scan and AI-assisted requests, keep saved business records available, restore subscription access and protect the service.

AI, dictation and scanned content

When you use Quote2Job Scan or AI-assisted note features, text or image-derived content needed for that request may be processed by service providers used to operate those features. Quote2Job presents a review step so you can check customer details, job information, quantities, prices and tax values before saving or sending them.

iPhone dictation and speech recognition may also involve Apple services according to your device settings and Apple's privacy terms.

Cloud services and storage

Quote2Job uses cloud services for authentication, database storage, file storage and selected app functions. Information is processed by those providers only as needed to operate the features you use. Quote2Job does not sell personal information and does not use your business records for third-party advertising.

Subscriptions and payments

Quote2Job Pro subscriptions are purchased through Apple. Apple processes payment information. Quote2Job does not receive your full payment-card or bank details. The app reads subscription status so paid access can be provided and restored.

Maps and addresses

When you choose address search, Quote2Job uses Apple Maps services to help find and complete addresses. Address information you select can then be saved with the relevant customer, business or job record.

Account deletion and retention

You can request permanent deletion of your Quote2Job account from Settings in the app. Account deletion removes the associated Quote2Job account data from the service, subject to any limited retention required for security, legal or compliance purposes. Deleting a Quote2Job account does not automatically cancel an Apple subscription; subscriptions are managed separately through your Apple Account.

Security and your rights

Quote2Job uses authenticated access and database access controls, although no online service can guarantee absolute security. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to certain processing of personal data.

Contact

Quote2Job is operated by Noel Carey in Ireland. For privacy questions or data requests, email careywebservicesireland@gmail.com.
