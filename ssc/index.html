Privacy Terms Support Privacy choices

Ask SSC

Type, speak, take a photo or add a PDF. SSC helps explain what you are working on at your level.

Study with a plan

Learn, Practice, Quiz and Exam modes help turn schoolwork into focused study sessions.

Organise school

Use your timetable, subjects, tests and assignments to work out what matters today and what is coming next.

Revision that remembers

SSC tracks academic progress and brings topics back when they are useful to revise.

Privacy & control

SSC does not sell student data or use it for behavioural advertising. AI features are optional, students can review learning memories, and they can permanently delete their account from the app.

Study support, not an official result: SSC can make mistakes. Practice marks and grades are estimates and are not official school or State Examination results.
