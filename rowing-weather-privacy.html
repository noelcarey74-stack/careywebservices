Rowing Weather

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Overview

Rowing Weather is designed to work without creating a user account. The app does not sell personal information, does not display third-party advertising and does not use personal data for tracking.

Location data

Rowing Weather can use your device location, or a location you choose on the map, to provide weather, wind, rain, course and tide-related information. Precise latitude and longitude may be sent to weather and marine data services only to return the requested information.

Location data is used for app functionality. It is not used by Rowing Weather for advertising, marketing, analytics or cross-app tracking, and Rowing Weather does not link it to a user account or identity.

Third-party services

Rowing Weather uses Apple Maps for mapping, Open-Meteo for weather forecast data, and a Supabase-hosted service for tide requests. These services may process technical request information in accordance with their own privacy and logging practices.

Optional profile name

You may enter an optional name in Profile & Settings. This name is stored only on your iPhone and is not uploaded to Rowing Weather servers or shared with weather providers.

Saved waters, courses and rigging

Saved waters, rowing courses, rigging setups and related app preferences are stored locally on your device. Rowing Weather does not require an online account to use these saved items.

Subscriptions and payments

Rowing Weather Pro subscriptions are purchased through Apple. Apple processes subscription payments and payment-card information. Rowing Weather does not receive your payment-card details.

Deleting local data

You can remove your optional name, saved waters, saved courses and saved rigging from Profile & Settings using Delete My Saved Data. Deleting local app data does not cancel an Apple subscription; subscriptions are managed through your Apple Account.

Children

Rowing Weather does not knowingly collect account information from children. The app does not contain social features, messaging or user-generated content sharing.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if Rowing Weather features or data practices change. The current version will remain available on this page.

Contact
