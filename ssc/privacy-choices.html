Your Privacy Choices

SSC gives students direct controls over their learning data.

HomePrivacy PolicySupport

Choose whether SSC can use AI

Open Learning Profile → Account & Privacy → AI data sharing. AI features only send new study information to OpenAI when you have explicitly allowed it. You can turn this choice off again at any time.

Review what SSC remembers

Open Learning Profile → What SSC knows about me to review learning-memory items and remove items you do not want SSC to remember.

Remove conversations

Use SSC's conversation history controls to remove conversations you no longer want kept in your account.

Change your school profile

Use Learning Profile to change school year, subjects, levels and other study settings.

Delete your whole account

Open Learning Profile → Account & Privacy, type DELETE, and confirm. Account deletion permanently removes the SSC Auth account and the associated learning data.

Ask for help

For privacy questions or help exercising a privacy right, email secondaryschoolcompanion@gmail.com.
