Privacy Policy

Updated 26 September 2026. SSC is built for students, so this policy is written to be understandable rather than hidden in legal language.

HomeTermsSupportPrivacy choices

What SSC stores

When you create an account, SSC may store your email address, name or nickname, school year, subjects and levels, timetable, assessments, study preferences, learning progress, revision information, conversations with SSC, learning-memory items, and official-exam practice history.

Photos, PDFs and schoolwork

If you choose to show SSC a photo or PDF, it is processed so SSC can help with the schoolwork. SSC does not currently use permanent file storage for the original upload. SSC may keep limited academic information created from it, such as a timetable extraction, short schoolwork summary or exam-practice record.

Why SSC uses your data

SSC uses your information to provide the study service you ask for: personalised explanations, study sessions, revision, timetable help, assessment planning, progress tracking, exam-style practice and useful academic memory.

AI processing

AI features are optional. SSC only sends new personal study information to OpenAI after you have chosen to allow AI data sharing. If you choose not to allow it, the non-AI parts of SSC remain available. You can change this choice later in Account & Privacy.

When AI sharing is allowed, SSC sends only what is needed for the feature being used. The AI provider may temporarily retain API inputs and outputs for safety and abuse monitoring under its current data-retention rules. Current standard OpenAI API documentation allows retention for up to 30 days unless different data controls apply.

Voice input

When you choose to speak, SSC uses Apple's microphone and Speech Recognition features to turn speech into text. SSC does not store the raw microphone recording in its own backend.

What SSC does not do

SSC does not sell student data, does not use student data for behavioural advertising, and is not designed to track students across other companies' apps or websites.

Your controls

You can review and delete learning-memory items, remove conversations, change subjects and profile settings, sign out, and permanently delete your account from Account & Privacy. Deleting the account removes the SSC account and associated learning data unless something must be retained by law.

Security

SSC uses authenticated accounts, encrypted network connections, device Keychain storage for the sign-in session, and database rules that separate one student's private data from another student's data.

Students and families

SSC is designed for secondary-school students and young people have privacy rights of their own. Younger students can ask a parent or guardian to read this information with them.

Contact

For privacy questions, access requests or help with your data, email secondaryschoolcompanion@gmail.com.
