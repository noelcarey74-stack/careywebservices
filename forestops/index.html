Need help with a job site, daily PDF, app account, cloud backup or subscription? This is the official support page for ForestOps Pro.

Contact support

Email careywebservicesireland@gmail.com with the issue, iPhone model, and app version. Please do not email passwords, sign-in codes, or private production reports unless necessary.

Getting started

Create or open a job site in the app. Record daily timber production by species, product and length, machine hours, supplies used or needed, and hydraulic hose repairs. Tap Save Daily Report to store work locally; use Save & Share PDF to send a printable report using the iPhone share sheet.

Using photographs

When recording a hose repair or supply request, tap Attach photo, select an image and check the preview. The photo is included with the saved report and its generated PDF. Remove unwanted photographs before saving.

Cloud backup and sign-in

Reports work offline on the iPhone. Optional cloud backup requires a ForestOps Pro account, which you can manage in Settings → Account & Backup. If you change phones, sign in to the same account and select the option to restore missing records before you start a new backup.

Subscriptions and billing

Creating, editing and sharing new reports requires an active ForestOps Pro subscription, purchased securely through Apple. Prices and introductory trial eligibility are displayed by Apple in the app. Restore an existing subscription with Restore Purchases. For cancellation, open Apple Subscriptions. Cancelling billing is separate from deleting a cloud-backup account.

Protecting reports

Check the report carefully before sharing. Sharing a PDF by Mail, Messages or WhatsApp sends it through the service you select; ForestOps Pro cannot recall a previously sent file. For privacy and account removal information, see our Privacy Policy and account deletion instructions.
