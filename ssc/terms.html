What SSC is

SSC is an AI study-support and school-organisation tool for secondary-school students. It can explain topics, build study sessions, help organise schoolwork, create practice and give estimated feedback.

Not an official result

SSC is not a school, teacher, the State Examinations Commission or another examination authority. Practice marks, percentages and grades are estimates for learning purposes only and are not official results.

AI can make mistakes

SSC is designed to be useful and curriculum-aware, but AI-generated information can still be wrong or incomplete. Important school requirements, deadlines and official exam rules should be checked with your teacher, school or the relevant official source.

Your account

Keep your password private and use your own account. Do not try to access another student's account, interfere with the service or use SSC to harm someone else.

Your schoolwork

Only upload material you are allowed to use. Avoid uploading unnecessary private information about other people.

Fair study use

SSC is intended to help you understand and learn. You remain responsible for following your school's rules about homework, projects, assessments and the use of AI.

Account deletion

You may permanently delete your SSC account from Account & Privacy. Once deletion completes, the account and associated SSC learning data cannot be restored.
