Common questions

Before you start.

Is AI Grind Ireland an official Department of Education app?

No. AI Grind Ireland is an independent educational study app. It is not an official Department of Education, NCCA or State Examinations Commission service.

Does AGI replace a teacher or grind tutor?

No. AGI is designed to support independent study, practice and revision. Important teaching or exam decisions should still be checked with teachers and official sources.

What does AGI remember?

AGI can save curriculum-topic progress, mastery, recent results and revision timing so later practice can focus on areas that need more work.

Can students listen instead of only reading?

Yes. The AGI Voice Tutor can read questions, explanations and feedback aloud. Automatic speaking is optional and off by default.

How are subscriptions managed?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Users can restore purchases in the app and manage or cancel subscriptions through their Apple Account.
