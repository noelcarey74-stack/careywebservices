Privacy Policy

This page explains how Carey Web Services collects, uses and protects information when customers contact us about simple websites for service businesses.

1. Who we are

Carey Web Services builds simple one-page websites for Irish service businesses, tradesmen, contractors, clubs, events and local services.

Website: careywebservices.ie
Email: careywebservicesireland@gmail.com

2. Information we may collect

When you contact Carey Web Services by phone, WhatsApp or email, we may collect information such as:

  • Your name
  • Your business name
  • Your phone number
  • Your WhatsApp number
  • Your email address
  • Your business services
  • Your business location or service area
  • Photos, logos or text you send for your website
  • Information needed to prepare a website quote or build your website

3. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Reply to your enquiry
  • Prepare a website quote
  • Build or update your website
  • Set up contact buttons, service details and website content
  • Help with domain setup or Google Business Profile details, where requested
  • Keep basic records of work completed and payments received

4. Legal basis for using your information

We use your information because you have contacted us about a service, asked for a quote, or requested website work. This is necessary so we can respond to you and provide the service.

5. Sharing your information

Carey Web Services does not sell your personal information.

We may need to use trusted third-party services when setting up or managing your website, such as:

  • Domain providers
  • Website hosting platforms
  • Google Business Profile tools
  • Email or communication services
  • Payment services such as Revolut, where used

6. Customer ownership of accounts

Customers should ideally own their own domain name and Google Business Profile. Carey Web Services may be added as a manager or helper where needed, but passwords should not be shared.

7. How long we keep information

We keep customer information only for as long as needed to reply to enquiries, complete website work, provide support, keep business records, and meet legal or tax obligations.

8. Your rights

You can contact Carey Web Services to ask about the information we hold about you. You may ask for information to be corrected or deleted where appropriate.

9. Website tracking and cookies

This website may use basic technical features needed for the website to work. If analytics, advertising pixels or extra tracking tools are added in the future, the cookie policy may be updated.

Carey Web Services currently focuses on simple service websites. This website is not an online shop and does not process online orders through this site.

10. Contact about privacy

For any privacy questions, contact Carey Web Services by email:

careywebservicesireland@gmail.com

11. Updates to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as the business, website or services change.
