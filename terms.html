1. About Carey Web Services

Carey Web Services builds simple websites and business extras for Irish service businesses, tradesmen, contractors, clubs, events and local services.

Website: careywebservices.ie
Email: careywebservicesireland@gmail.com

2. What Carey Web Services provides

Carey Web Services provides simple website and business support services such as:

  • One-page service websites
  • Service business website layouts
  • Call, WhatsApp and email buttons
  • Social media buttons and links
  • Website wording and basic Google search wording
  • Photo and gallery sections
  • Domain setup help
  • Google Business Profile help where requested
  • QR code business cards
  • Google review cards
  • Printable PDF flyers or simple business designs

3. What Carey Web Services does not provide

Carey Web Services does not currently build:

  • Online shops

  • Large custom web applications
  • Websites requiring constant product changes
Carey Web Services focuses on service websites and simple business extras only. This keeps the work simple, clear and suitable for small local businesses.
